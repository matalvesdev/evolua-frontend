# Paid Media — Evolua

## Core Responsibilities
Convert budget into qualified leads and customers. Manage Evolua's paid presence across Google, Meta, LinkedIn, and emerging channels. Optimize for ROAS while maintaining brand quality.

## Channel Strategy

### Google Ads
- **Search**: Brand + non-brand keywords for fonoaudiologia software
  - Brand: Defend Evolua brand terms
  - Non-brand: High-intent ("software para fonoaudiologia", "prontuário digital fono")
  - Dynamic Search Ads: Cover long-tail queries
- **Display**: Retargeting website visitors + prospecting on health/clinical sites
- **YouTube**: Educational video ads (in-stream + discovery)
- **PMax**: Full-funnel automated campaigns (once conversion data is mature)

### Meta Ads (Facebook + Instagram)
- **TOFU**: Video views + reach campaigns for cold audiences
  - Creative: Educational reels, tip carousels, problem-awareness videos
  - Audience: Interest-based (fonoaudiologia, clínica, saúde) + lookalikes
- **MOFU**: Traffic + engagement for warm audiences
  - Creative: Testimonials, case studies, feature demos
  - Audience: Website visitors, video viewers (50%), social engagers
- **BOFU**: Conversions for hot audiences
  - Creative: Free trial offers, consultation bookings, limited-time
  - Audience: Pricing page visitors, high-intent pages, add-to-cart
- **Retention**: Past trial users who didn't convert, lapsed customers

### LinkedIn Ads
- **Purpose**: B2B targeting for clinic owners and group practices
- **Formats**: Sponsored content (thought leadership), InMail (events), lead gen forms
- **Audience**: Job title targeting (fonoaudióloga, proprietário de clínica, terapeuta)
- **Budget**: 10-15% of total paid budget

## Campaign Structure

### Full-Funnel Framework
```
TOFU (40% budget) — Awareness
  Meta: Video views, reach
  Google: YouTube, Display
  LinkedIn: Sponsored content

MOFU (30% budget) — Consideration  
  Meta: Traffic, engagement
  Google: Search (non-brand)
  LinkedIn: Lead gen (content download)

BOFU (25% budget) — Conversion
  Meta: Conversions (trial signup, demo)
  Google: Search (brand + high-intent), PMax
  LinkedIn: Conversion (trial, consultation)

Retention (5% budget)
  Meta: Engagement with existing leads
  Google: Brand defense
```

## Creative Testing Framework

### Testing Cadence
- **Weekly**: 1 new creative per ad set (Meta), 3 new RSAs per ad group (Google)
- **Monthly**: Creative audit — pause bottom 20% performers
- **Quarterly**: Full creative refresh — new concepts, hooks, formats

### What to Test
- Hooks: Question vs. Bold claim vs. Data vs. Story
- Formats: Video vs. Carousel vs. Single image
- Length: Short copy vs. Long copy
- CTA: "Teste grátis" vs. "Agende uma demo" vs. "Saiba mais"
- Offers: Free trial vs. Discount vs. Content download

### Fatigue Detection
Monitor: Frequency >3 (Meta), Impression share declining, CTR dropping >20%, CPA rising >20% — pause and rotate creative immediately.

## Conversion Tracking Requirements

### Google Ads
- Enhanced conversions enabled
- All valuable actions tracked: trial signup, demo request, lead form, phone call
- Offline conversion import (lead-to-customer tracking)
- Attribution model: Data-driven

### Meta Ads
- Meta Pixel + CAPI (Conversions API) for server-side tracking
- Standard events: ViewContent, InitiateCheckout, Lead, CompleteRegistration
- Custom conversions for trial signups
- Offline event tracking

### LinkedIn Ads
- Insight Tag installed on all pages
- Conversion tracking for lead gen forms + website actions

## Budget Management

### Allocation Guidelines
- **40%** Meta Ads (primary growth channel)
- **35%** Google Ads (intent capture + retargeting)
- **15%** LinkedIn (B2B + clinic owners)
- **10%** Testing budget (new channels, formats)

### Optimization Cadence
- **Daily**: Budget pacing, pause underperformers, scale winners
- **Weekly**: Bid adjustments, audience refinement, creative swaps
- **Biweekly**: Full account audit (structure, keywords, audiences)
- **Monthly**: Budget reallocation across channels
