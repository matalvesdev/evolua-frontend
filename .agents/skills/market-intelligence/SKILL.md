# Market Intelligence — Evolua

## Core Responsibilities
Monitor the market, competitors, customers, and trends to produce actionable intelligence. The foundation of everything Evolua Marketing does.

## Research Sources

### Customer Voice
- Reclame Aqui
- Procon
- Google Reviews (clinics, competitors, Evolua)
- App Store Reviews (iOS)
- Play Store Reviews (Android)
- Customer support tickets (Evolua internal)

### Social Listening
- Reddit (r/fonoaudiologia, r/psicologia, r/autismo)
- LinkedIn (speech therapy groups)
- Instagram (comments on competitor/fono influencer posts)
- Facebook Groups (fonoaudiólogas do Brasil, clínicas de fono)
- YouTube comments (fono channels)

### Competitor Intelligence
- Competitor websites and blogs
- Pricing pages
- Product changelogs
- G2, Capterra (if applicable)
- Job postings (hiring signals = investment areas)
- Social media presence (frequency, engagement)
- Ad library (Meta Ad Library, Google Ads transparency)

### Trend Monitoring
- Google Trends (Brazil, healthcare, speech therapy)
- ANS regulatory updates
- CFC (Conselho Federal de Fonoaudiologia) publications
- Academic papers (SciELO, PubMed — speech therapy tech)
- Startup funding (Brazil healthtech, Kangu, etc.)
- International benchmarks (US speech therapy SaaS)

## Agent Workflows

### Workflow A: Competitor Deep-Dive
1. Identify 3-5 direct competitors (iClinic, Ninsaúde, Simples Dental, Holmed)
2. Analyze each across: product, pricing, positioning, content, reviews, social, ads
3. Generate SWOT for each competitor
4. Identify gaps (features, content, audience segments)
5. Produce Battle Card (1-pager per competitor)
6. Recommend offensive and defensive plays

**Output**: `Competitor Battle Card - [Competitor Name].md`

### Workflow B: Voice of Customer Research
1. Define research question (e.g., "What frustrates fonoaudiólogas about clinic management software?")
2. Gather data from review sources + social listening
3. Categorize pain points by theme (billing, scheduling, documentation, etc.)
4. Quantify frequency and sentiment per theme
5. Map pain points to Evolua features/content opportunities
6. Produce VOC Synthesis document

**Output**: `VOC Synthesis - [Theme].md`

### Workflow C: Persona Creation
1. Segment by: role (fonoaudióloga autônoma, clinic owner, psicóloga, terapeuta ocupacional)
2. For each segment, define:
   - Demographics
   - Goals and motivations
   - Pain points and frustrations
   - Information sources
   - Decision criteria
   - Objections to Evolua
3. Validate personas with real customer interviews if possible
4. Produce Persona Card

**Output**: `Persona - [Segment Name].md`

### Workflow D: Trend Alert
1. Scan all trend sources weekly
2. Identify signal (new regulation, technology, competitor move, market shift)
3. Assess impact on Evolua (opportunity or threat)
4. Score urgency (now/next quarter/this year/watch)
5. Produce Trend Alert (1-paragraph summary + recommendation)

**Output**: `Trend Alert - [Topic].md`
