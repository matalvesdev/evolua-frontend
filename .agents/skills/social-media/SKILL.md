# Social Media — Evolua

## Core Responsibilities
Distribute content across platforms, build community, drive engagement, amplify brand authority. Every post must serve a purpose: educate, inspire, or convert.

## Platform Strategy

### Instagram (@useevolua)
- **Purpose**: Brand awareness, education, community
- **Content mix**: 40% educational carousels, 30% reels (tips, day-in-clinic), 20% stories (polls, Q&A, behind-the-scenes), 10% community (reposts, user spotlights)
- **Tone**: Warm, professional, aspirational
- **Hashtags**: 15-20 per post, mix of volume tiers (fonoaudiologia, clinicadefonoaudiologia, softwaredesaude)
- **Cadence**: 1 feed post/day, 3-5 stories/day, 3-4 reels/week
- **Key tactic**: Carousel strategy — educational hooks that save-worthy content

### LinkedIn (Evolua company page + founder personal brand)
- **Purpose**: B2B authority, partnerships, talent, thought leadership
- **Content mix**: 50% thought leadership (industry insights, data), 25% product (features, case studies), 15% culture (team, values), 10% community (engagement posts)
- **Tone**: Professional, data-driven, generous with insights
- **Cadence**: 1 post/day (Mon-Fri), founder posts 2-3x/week
- **Key tactic**: Document carousels — PDF posts get 3x engagement

### Facebook
- **Purpose**: Community groups, older demographic reach
- **Content mix**: 40% educational content, 30% group engagement (fonoaudiólogas groups), 20% events/webinars, 10% promotions
- **Tone**: Friendly, conversational, supportive
- **Cadence**: 1 post/day, active in 5+ fono groups
- **Key tactic**: Facebook Groups — join and contribute meaningfully, don't just post links

### TikTok (if applicable)
- **Purpose**: Reach younger fonoaudiólogas, students, Gen Z patients' families
- **Content**: Reels repurposed + platform-native trends
- **Tone**: Authentic, fun, behind-the-scenes
- **Key tactic**: Educational trends — duet/stitch with trending audio

## Copywriting Framework

### Hook (first 1-3 lines)
- Question: "Você sabe qual o maior erro na precificação de sessões?"
- Bold statement: "80% das fonoaudiólogas cobram menos do que deveriam."
- Relatable: "Se você já perdeu um paciente por causa da agenda, esse post é pra você."
- Data: "Estudo mostra que clínicas com prontuário digital faturam 40% mais."

### Body
- 3-5 key points
- Bullet-friendly formatting for mobile
- White space between paragraphs
- Emojis used sparingly and purposefully (✅📌💡)

### CTA
- Engagement: "Comenta aqui: qual sua maior dificuldade?"
- Save: "Salva esse post pra consultar depois."
- Share: "Marca aquela amiga fono que precisa ver isso."
- Convert: "Link na bio para teste gratuito."

## Community Management

### Daily Tasks
- Reply to ALL comments within 2 hours (business hours)
- Reply to ALL DMs within 4 hours
- Engage with 10-15 relevant accounts (likes, comments, shares)
- Monitor branded hashtags
- Save UGC for repost opportunities

### Response Guidelines
- Positive comment: Thank + add value + engage
- Question: Answer fully + offer to continue via DM
- Negative comment: Acknowledge publicly + move to DM
- Troll/spam: Delete + block (never engage)
- Customer issue: Tag support team

### Community Growth Tactics
- Weekly Q&A stories (ask me anything about clinic management)
- User spotlight Fridays (highlight a fono using Evolua)
- Educational challenges (e.g., "7 dias de organização financeira na clínica")
- Collaborations with fono influencers (micro-influencers: 5k-50k followers)
- Ambassador program (power users who share authentic content)

## Key Metrics

| Platform | Primary Metrics | Secondary Metrics |
|----------|----------------|-------------------|
| Instagram | Saves, shares, profile visits | Reach, engagement rate, follower growth |
| LinkedIn | Engagement rate, profile visits, DM inquiries | Followers, post impressions, reposts |
| Facebook | Group engagement, link clicks | Reach, comments, shares |
| TikTok | Completion rate, shares | Views, followers, profile clicks |

## Weekly Review
- What worked? (top 3 posts by engagement)
- What didn't? (bottom 3 by engagement — why?)
- Platform trends worth adapting?
- Competitor social moves to respond to?
- Community sentiment shifts?
