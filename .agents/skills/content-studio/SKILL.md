# Content Studio — Evolua

Transform market intelligence into valuable, findable, shareable content. Build Evolua's topical authority across all stages of the customer journey.

## Editorial Philosophy
- **Educational first**: Every piece teaches something useful
- **Customer language**: Speak like a fonoaudióloga, not like a tech company
- **Evidence-based**: Reference research, data, and clinical guidelines
- **Actionable**: Reader should finish with something they can apply today
- **SEO-native**: Structure content for Google and AI search (AEO/GEO)

## SEO & Discoverability Standards

### Blog Post Structure
```
Title (H1): Include primary keyword naturally
  Meta Description: 150-160 chars, include keyword + CTA
  URL slug: lowercase, hyphens, keyword-rich
  Featured image: 1200x628px with alt text
  H2 sections: Subtopics with secondary keywords
    H3 subsections: Deep dives
  Internal links: 3-5 links to related Evolua content
  External links: 2-3 authoritative sources (SciELO, PubMed, CFC)
  CTA: One primary CTA (ebook download, free trial, newsletter)
```

### AI Search Optimization (AEO/GEO)
- Write passages of 134-167 words that can stand alone as answers
- Use question-based H2s: "How does teleconsultation work for fono?"
- Include structured data (FAQ schema where applicable)
- Cite sources with specific findings — LLMs prefer attribution
- Define terms explicitly: "Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC)..."

### Keyword Strategy
- Primary: Medium-low competition, high relevance (e.g., "software para fonoaudiologia")
- Secondary: Long-tail, question-based (e.g., "como emitir guias de sessão fono")
- Tertiary: Topical support (e.g., "TISS", "CFC", "códigos de procedimento fono")
- Cluster: Link all posts within a topic silo

## Content Types & Formats

### Blog Posts
- Length: 1500-2500 words
- Frequency: 5x week (Mon-Fri)
- Format: SEO-optimized, HTML with proper heading hierarchy
- CTA: Lead magnet, free trial, newsletter signup

### Ebooks
- Length: 5000-10000 words
- Topics: Research-backed guides solving a specific problem
- Gate: Email capture (Lead Magnet)
- Design: Professional layout with Evolua branding
- Distribution: Landing page + social + email + ads

### Newsletters
- Cadence: Weekly (Sunday digest)
- Structure: 1 main article + 2 curated links + 1 Evolua update
- Tone: Friendly, personal, value-first
- CTA: Reply, share, book demo

### Lead Magnets
- Allowed: Ebooks, guides, infographics, mini-courses, templates
- Disallowed: Spreadsheets, checklists, form templates
- Catalog: 6 materials (ebook-whatsapp-profissional, ebook-tendencias, ebook-protocolos, ebook-mkt-digital-fono, infografico-marcos-fala, infografico-montar-clinica)

## Content Repurposing Pipeline

Every blog post generates:
1. LinkedIn post (hook + 3 key takeaways + link)
2. Instagram carousel (5-8 slides summarizing main points)
3. Twitter/X thread (10-15 tweets expanding on key ideas)
4. 2-3 Stories (question polls, "saved" stickers)
5. Newsletter excerpt
6. Video script (30-60 sec Reel/TikTok)
7. Audio snippet (podcast teaser if applicable)

## Quality Gate
Before any content is published:
1. SEO check (keywords, meta, headings, internal links)
2. Brand voice check (tone, terminology, CTA alignment)
3. Accuracy check (clinical claims, citations, data)
4. Legal check (regulatory compliance for healthcare)
5. Formatting check (mobile-readiness, images, readability)

## Key Metrics
- Organic traffic growth (MoM)
- Keyword rankings (top 3, top 10, top 30)
- Content-to-lead conversion rate
- Newsletter open rate / CTR
- Ebook downloads
- Time on page / bounce rate
- AI citation rate (brand mentions in LLM outputs)
