# Competitor Profile: Simples Dental (Benchmark)

**Date:** 2026-05-22
**Domain:** simplesdental.com.br

---

## Overview

Simples Dental is the leading dental practice management software in Latin America. 80k+ users. Best-in-class UX benchmark. Not a direct competitor (dentistry, not speech therapy), but the closest analog to what Evolua is doing for fonoaudiologia. Reference of how verticalization works.

---

## Positioning

**Tagline:** "Simplifique sua gestão com o sistema mais bem avaliado do mercado"
**ICP:** Dentists, dental clinics of all sizes
**Core promise:** Simplify management with excellent UX
**Reclame Aqui:** 9.2/10 (best in class)

---

## Pricing

- R$137–R$350/mês per clinic (NOT per professional) — better for multi-professional
- Pricing by clinic (not by user) — smart bundling
- 7-day free trial

---

## Content Strategy

**Blog:** simplesdental.com/blog
**Topics:** Dental marketing, practice management, technology for dentists, patient experience, clinical tips
**Frequency:** ~4-8 posts/month
**Format:** Blog articles, infographics, some video
**Angle:** Educational + promotional — helps dentists run better practices while showcasing the product
**YouTube:** Active
**Social:** Active Instagram, LinkedIn
**Partner content:** Dental Cremer (Henry Schein) distribution partnership

**Key takeaway:** Strong content marketing engine. Focus on marketing for dentists and practice growth. Brand is synonymous with "simplicity."

---

## Strengths

- Best UX in Brazilian healthcare software (81k+ users, Reclame Aqui 9.2)
- Pricing per clinic (not per professional) — better alignment
- Strong brand: "Simples" = simplicity promise delivered
- Content marketing engine
- Partner ecosystem (Dental Cremer, Asaas, ZapSign)

## Weaknesses (for our context)

- Dental-only — can't serve speech therapists
- Their success validates the verticalization thesis for Evolua
- Will take time/build to reach their brand authority level
