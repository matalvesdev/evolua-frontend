## Strategic Notes

1. **Cluster approach:** Content should be organized in topic clusters around keywords (prontuário fono, gestão de clínicas fono, WhatsApp fono)
2. **Repurpose:** Blog posts → Carrossel IG + Reels snippets + LinkedIn articles
3. **Seasonal:** Align with CFFa renewal periods (Nov-Dec), back-to-school (Jan-Feb), clinical conference calendar
4. **Competitive keywords with low difficulty:** "sistema para fonoaudióloga" (low difficulty, low volume but very high intent)
5. **Go-to-market priority:** Start with #1 (protocolos nativos — strongest differentiation) + #4 (diferenciação vs concorrentes — positions the market)
