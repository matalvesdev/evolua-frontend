# Competitor Profile: Holmed

**Date:** 2026-05-22
**Domain:** holmed.com.br

---

## Overview

Holmed is a clinical management software focused on physiotherapy, nutrition, and psychology. Older product with limited modern features. Present in the rehabilitation and therapy segment.

---

## Positioning

**ICP:** Physiotherapists, nutritionists, psychologists — therapy professionals
**Notable:** One of the few that partially overlaps with the speech therapy adjacent market

---

## Content Strategy

**Presence:** Low digital presence. No active blog or consistent content marketing.
**Social media:** Not prominent.
**YouTube/Video:** No relevant presence.

---

## Strengths

- Established brand in physiotherapy segment
- Covers a therapy-adjacent market

## Weaknesses

- Product is outdated, no modern UX
- No mobile app of quality
- No AI features
- No speech therapy specialization
- No content marketing strategy
- Low digital presence
