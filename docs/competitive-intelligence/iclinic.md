# Competitor Profile: iClinic (Afya)

**Date:** 2026-05-22
**Domain:** iclinic.com.br
**Parent:** Afya (NASDAQ: AFYA) — acquired

---

## Overview

iClinic is the market leader in clinical management software for general medicine in Brazil. Founded 2012 in Ribeirão Preto, SP. Acquired by Afya (medical education group). ~38,906 active payers (Clinical Management segment, Dec 2025). 102+ employees. Revenue engine for Afya's Medical Practice Solutions (R$171M in 2025).

---

## Positioning

**Tagline:** "Software médico online para clínicas e consultórios"
**Core promise:** Facilitate medical practice by organizing information, increasing productivity, and maximizing revenue.
**ICP:** General physicians, medical clinics of all sizes. NOT specialized for any single specialty.

---

## Pricing

- R$99–R$299/mês/profissional
- Charged per professional, not per clinic — becomes expensive for multi-professional clinics
- Reputação Reclame Aqui: 6.5/10

---

## Content Strategy

**Blog:** blog.iclinic.com.br (general medical practice content)
**Topics:** Practice management, medical marketing, technology for clinics, LGPD, recepcionist workflows
**Frequency:** ~2-4 posts/month
**Format:** Blog articles (text), some infographics
**Angle:** Educational/managerial — focused on helping physicians run their offices better
**LinkedIn:** 26k followers (now part of Afya, LinkedIn profile shows "desativado")
**YouTube:** Content via Afya's education channels

**Key gap:** No specialized content for speech therapy. Zero content on MBGR, DOSS, GRBAS, FOIS, or any fono-specific protocol.

---

## Strengths

- Market leader with largest user base
- Backed by Afya (public company, NASDAQ) — financial stability
- Broad feature set for general medicine
- Strong brand recognition among Brazilian physicians
- Integration with Afya ecosystem (Whitebook, Papers, education)

## Weaknesses

- **No specialization for speech therapy** — generic medical EMR adapted
- Charged per professional (expensive for small clinics)
- Support complaints (Reclame Aqui 6.5/10, reports of 20-day response)
- Fono-specific protocols (MBGR, DOSS, FOIS, GRBAS, VHI-10) would need to be manually recreated in free text fields
- iClinic is a generalist product trying to serve all specialties
