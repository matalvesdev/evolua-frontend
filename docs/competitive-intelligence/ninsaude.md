# Competitor Profile: Ninsaúde Clinic

**Date:** 2026-05-22
**Domain:** ninsaude.com.br
**Also operates:** apolo.app (blog/platform)

---

## Overview

Ninsaúde Clinic is a multi-specialty clinical management system targeting medium to large clinics (20+ professionals). Offers EHR, scheduling, financial management, CRM, inventory, and patient engagement. Positioned for clinics that need a comprehensive solution. Pricing starts at ~R$199/mês. Also offers Ninsaúde Pay, Ninsaúde Sign, Ninsaúde Safe (audio recording).

---

## Positioning

**Tagline:** "Sistema para clínicas — Perfeito para clínicas com mais de 20 médicos"
**ICP:** Multi-professional clinics, medium-large scale. Specialties: cardiology, plastic surgery, pediatrics, ophthalmology, orthopedics, gastroenterology, dentistry, physiotherapy.
**Notable:** Has a white-label franchise model.

---

## Pricing

- From R$199/mês (for larger clinics)
- Free trial available
- Ninsaúde Pay (payment processing)
- International presence (English, Spanish versions)

---

## Content Strategy

**Blog:** blog.apolo.app
**Topics:** Clinical management, marketing for clinics, technology in healthcare, software comparison, opening a practice
**Frequency:** ~8-12 posts/month (high output)
**Format:** Blog articles (long-form, 1500+ words), some with audio versions
**Angle:** Educational/authority — positions Ninsaúde as a thought leader in clinical management
**YouTube:** Not prominent
**LinkedIn:** Active

**Key gap:** No speech therapy specialization. Focused on medical/physiotherapy/dentistry. No fono-specific protocols.

---

## Strengths

- Comprehensive feature set (EHR + CRM + Finance + Inventory + Pay + Sign)
- White-label model for franchises
- International presence (US, Spanish markets)
- Active blog with high content output
- API for developers

## Weaknesses

- Premium pricing (R$199+)
- Targeted at medium-large clinics (20+ professionals) — overshooting small fono practices
- No specialization for speech therapy
- Multi-specialty but no deep expertise in any single one
- Complex for solo practitioners
