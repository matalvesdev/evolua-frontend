## Strategic Insight

**There is NO dedicated, consolidated software for speech therapy in Brazil with relevant traction.** This confirms Evolua's market vacuum thesis — analogous to what Simples Dental did for dentistry.

The closest "competitors" are generalist medical EMRs (iClinic, Ninsaúde, Holmed) that serve fonoaudiólogas poorly — they have to manually recreate MBGR, DOSS, FOIS, GRBAS, VHI-10 in free text fields.

---

## Key Content Gaps (Opportunities)

1. **Fono-specific educational content:** No competitor creates content about speech therapy practice management
2. **Protocol templates:** No competitor offers native MBGR, DOSS, FOIS, GRBAS, VHI-10
3. **WhatsApp integration:** No competitor offers native WhatsApp for scheduling, reminders, billing
4. **AI-assisted reporting:** No competitor generates therapy reports via AI
5. **Brazilian payments:** No competitor offers native PIX recurring billing
